# Welcome! Servus! Bonjour! Hola!

I am a PhD student in mathematical physics, specifically focused on Quantum Walks and their long-time dynamics.

I am currently finishing my PhD at Université Grenoble Alpes.

## Research interests

My research interests include:

- Dynamical localization, in particular the Aizenman-Molchanov method, fractional moment estimates and eigenfunction correlators
- Topological invariants and their relation to transport
- The effects of disorder induced by randomness
- Open quantum systems

## PhD Thesis

My PhD project is titled "Random Quantum Walks, Transport and Out-of-Equilibirum Dynamics". The defense will take place on the 15th of December and the manuscript will be available here shortly.
